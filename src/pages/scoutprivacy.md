# /scout Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 19, 2026**

/scout is a robotics scouting and match-prediction app maintained by Carson Loy for use by FRC Team 5817. In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the app's operator. This policy explains how information is collected, used, shared, and retained when you use the app or contact us about it.

For privacy questions or requests, email **[scoutprivacy@carsonloy.com](mailto:scoutprivacy@carsonloy.com)**.

## Information we collect

### Account information

When you register, you provide your first name, last name, email address, and password. Google Firebase Authentication processes your password and creates an account identifier. The app stores your name, email address, account identifier where needed, account permissions, and preferences such as your selected theme and scouting settings. Your password is not stored in your scouting profile or Google Sheets.

### Scouting and team activity

We collect information you enter or generate while using the app, including:

- Selected events, match numbers, robot team numbers, and scouting assignments.
- Match observations, scoring counts, robot performance, autonomous paths you draw, and written notes.
- Your name and submission metadata, such as account identifiers, email address, submission identifiers, and timestamps, used to attribute entries and prevent duplicate submissions.
- Scout rosters, schedules, assigned slots, and related team coordination information.
- Match predictions, credits used, results, credit balances and activity, theme purchases, and leaderboard statistics.

Team administrators may also import existing scouting records from CSV files and create or update schedules, permissions, and credit balances. Those records may include information about scouts who did not personally enter it into the app.

The paths drawn on the field image describe robot movement. They are not measurements of your device's location. Please keep written notes relevant to robotics scouting and avoid including sensitive personal information about yourself or others.

### Technical information and communications

Our service providers may process IP addresses, client or device information, service-generated identifiers, request timestamps, and operational or security logs when the app connects to their services. Diagnostic output may include submission details and error information.

Password-reset requests are subject to abuse prevention. We store hashed versions of the requested email address and requesting IP address, along with request times and counts. Hashing does not make these records anonymous.

If you contact us, request account or scouting-data deletion, or use password recovery, we process the information needed to handle that request. This can include your email address, name, account identifier, message, request choices, timestamps, and email delivery records.

## How we use information

We use this information to:

- Create accounts, authenticate users, maintain preferences, and provide password recovery.
- Collect and organize scouting observations, analyze robot performance, and support team planning.
- Attribute observations to scouts so the team can review data quality, provide feedback, and train scouts.
- Coordinate scouting schedules and slot assignments.
- Operate match predictions, in-app credits, themes, results, and leaderboards.
- Prevent duplicate submissions, misuse, and unauthorized account activity; investigate technical problems; and maintain the app.
- Respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests and send related service emails.

The app does not collect payment-card or bank-account details. Its credits are in-app values used for the app's features.

## Who can access information

### Other app users and team administrators

The app is intended for shared team use. App records should not be treated as confidential from other signed-in users. Account and team records may be accessible to them, including names, email addresses, scouting observations, assignments, prediction activity, and credit statistics. Team administrators also use account-management, schedule, credit, and data import/export tools.

Scouting entries can be copied into team-managed spreadsheets, exported as CSV files, or shared through QR codes. Anyone who receives an export, a QR code, or access to a spreadsheet can access the information it contains. Removing information from the app does not automatically remove copies already shared outside our control.

### Service providers

We use the following services to operate the app and handle communications:

- **Google Firebase and Google Cloud:** account authentication, database storage, backend functions, and operational infrastructure. These services process account information, app records, and related technical information. See [Firebase's privacy and security information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
- **Google Sheets and Google email services:** team-managed scouting spreadsheets and administrative communications. Scouting uploads to Sheets include your displayed scout name and match observations; they do not include your account password. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Resend:** delivery of password-reset and account-deletion emails. Resend processes recipient email addresses, message contents, and delivery information. Password-reset messages contain a reset link; deletion notifications may contain your name, email address, account identifier, and deletion request. See [Resend's Privacy Policy](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy).
- **The Blue Alliance:** retrieval of public competition, team, alliance, and match information. Requests include event identifiers and ordinary network information. The app does not send your account password or scouting notes to The Blue Alliance. See [The Blue Alliance's Privacy Policy](https://www.thebluealliance.com/privacy).
- **Email routing and mailbox providers, including Cloudflare:** forwarding and handling messages sent to our privacy contact address. These providers process the message and associated email information to deliver it. See [Cloudflare's Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

We share information with providers for the purposes described in this policy, subject to their applicable service and data-protection terms. Providers processing personal information on our behalf must provide protection consistent with this policy and applicable law, including appropriate confidentiality, security, and limits on use. Providers may also process technical information under their own applicable privacy policies. We may disclose information when required by law, to respond to a lawful request, or to protect users and the service from misuse.

We do not sell personal information or share it with advertising networks for targeted advertising. The app does not display advertisements or use tracking intended to follow you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Information stored on your device

The app and its Firebase services may store sign-in state, preferences, selected events, cached competition data, and copies of database records on your device. If you use the normal Cache feature, your scouting entry is saved locally until you upload or remove it.

Signing out does not necessarily erase every locally cached record or entry. You can manage pending entries through Cached Uploads in Settings. Clearing the app's stored data or uninstalling it generally removes its local data, subject to your device's backup and restore behavior. This does not delete records already stored online, exported, or copied into Google Sheets.

## Demo mode

For an account with Demo mode enabled, scouting upload and cache actions prepare the entry through the usual interface and then discard it. These actions do not save scouting entries to Firestore, Google Sheets, or the offline upload queue. Demo QR previews do not contain exportable scouting observations. The current form may still hold your input in memory until you submit or reset it, and diagnostic output may contain submission details.

Demo mode is not anonymous. Your account, account-specific simulated prediction events, predictions, real in-app credit balance, theme purchases, and related history are still stored and processed. Demo mode does not remove information saved before the mode was enabled.

## Retention and account deletion

We retain account information while your account is active. Scouting records and their attribution may be kept across competition events and seasons to support historical analysis, data quality review, and scout training, unless you request their removal.

### Delete your sign-in account

You can request deletion through **Settings → Delete my account**. Once deletion succeeds, your Firebase sign-in account and its private profile records, including the personal credit wallet and private prediction history, are removed and you are signed out. You cannot sign in with that deleted account.

Account deletion does not automatically erase existing scouting entries, shared prediction or leaderboard records, shared assignments or schedules, administrative records, or previously exported copies. These records may contain your name or other account information. Do not assume deleting your sign-in account removes every record associated with you.

### Request removal of your scouting entries

To request scouting-entry removal when deleting your account, select **“Delete all of my scouting entries”** in the deletion dialog. This records a request and notifies the administrator; it does not immediately delete the entries.

**We will manually remove the requested scouting entries from the active app database, team-managed Google Sheets, and team-managed exports within 30 days of your request.** If you leave the checkbox unchecked, your existing scouting entries, including your displayed name, remain available to the team.

You can also email **[scoutprivacy@carsonloy.com](mailto:scoutprivacy@carsonloy.com)** to request removal of scouting entries or personal information remaining in shared predictions, schedules, assignments, or other records. You do not need an active account to make a request. We may ask for information needed to verify which account or records belong to you. Please do not send your password or a password-reset link.

### Records that can remain

We retain deletion-request records, related email delivery records, and operational or abuse-prevention records as needed to document requests, maintain the service, and address security issues or legal obligations. Deletion-request records can include your name, email address, account identifier, request choices, and timestamps. They are not automatically erased by deleting your sign-in account. You can contact us about personal information retained in these records.

The 30-day scouting-removal period applies to the active systems and team-managed copies we control. Provider backups and security records may remain for additional periods under the providers' retention policies. Copies independently retained by other recipients are outside our direct control. Information that no longer identifies you may be retained for aggregate or historical analysis.

## Your choices and requests

You can change available preferences in Settings, choose what optional notes to submit, manage pending cached entries, stop using the app, and request account or data deletion.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access or receive a copy of personal information, correct inaccurate information, request deletion, or object to or restrict certain uses. Contact **[scoutprivacy@carsonloy.com](mailto:scoutprivacy@carsonloy.com)** to make a request. We respond subject to applicable law and the need to verify the requester's identity. Where applicable, you may raise a concern with your local data-protection authority.

## Students and children

The app is intended for robotics team members and mentors, including teenage students. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact **[scoutprivacy@carsonloy.com](mailto:scoutprivacy@carsonloy.com)** so we can investigate and arrange removal.

## Security and processing locations

Firebase handles account authentication, and connections to Firebase, Resend, and The Blue Alliance use encrypted HTTPS connections. Locally cached entries use ordinary app storage and may be included in device backups. No method of storage or transmission provides an absolute security guarantee.

Information may be stored or processed in the United States and other countries where our providers operate. Their locations may differ from where you live.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or its data practices change. We will update the date above and provide additional notice when required by applicable law. Any consent required for a new use will be requested separately.
